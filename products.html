ConcreteIQ

AI that bids concrete.

ConcreteIQ reads a project's drawing set and prices the concrete scope — foundations, slabs, elevated decks, tilt-up panels — so estimators spend their time on judgment instead of takeoff. It's built to change how concrete contractors win work.

  • Caught $144,219 of tilt-up panel steel missing from a hand-priced bid on a live $1.67M school project — the bid was re-issued $165,000 higher
  • Built alongside a practicing concrete estimator and tested against real bids
  • Refuses to finalize a bid while any line is unpriced, so nothing slips through
  • Early beta feedback from established concrete contractors has been strongly positive

Brickture

Snap it. Build it. Order the kit.

Photograph a real-world object — a car, a mug, a fire hydrant — and Brickture designs a brick version that stands up on its own, then gives you everything you need to build it: quality step-by-step instructions, a parts list, and kit ordering built right into the app.

  • Step-by-step instructions from the first brick to the last, with a parts list and estimated cost
  • Kit ordering built into the app — pick a build and order its parts as a kit (in beta)
  • Box-art-quality pictures of every build, generated from your photo (in testing)
  • A rotatable 3D model that assembles itself on screen, about 90 seconds after you take the photo

Hoof Harted

Somebody dealt it. The unit will decide who.

A party scan game. Everyone's names go into the phone, a preposterously over-engineered detector runs its sweep, and the verdict screen names the culprit — complete with a confidence score and a margin of error of “total.” It senses nothing. That is the entire joke.

  • Three scan types — Smell Scan, Smart Scan, and Funny Scan: who dealt it, who's smartest, who's funniest
  • Film-scanner sound design and haptics — every sound synthesized in code
  • No account, no sign-up, no login — the names stay on your phone
  • Pro adds Rigged Job (pick the culprit in advance — it's a toy, you're allowed to rig it), your own scans, your own music, scan lengths from 3 to 60 seconds, and unlimited scans

True Record

Hear the documents that run the country.

True Record narrates official US government documents — Supreme Court opinions, House and Senate committee reports, and Congressional Research Service reports — read in full, in their own words. No panel. No commentary. No spin. The document, start to finish.

  • Supreme Court opinions in full — the syllabus, the majority, and every concurrence and dissent, each attributed and each its own chapter
  • Read along — a transcript that highlights the sentence being spoken
  • Follow the Court, a committee, or a topic and get a plain factual alert the morning a document lands
  • Offline downloads, playback speed, and lock-screen and headphone controls for the commute

Ann Elise

AI tutors that actually teach.

Ann Elise is our AI tutor for college Organic Chemistry, built on one principle: teach, don't answer. Like your best TA, she asks the right questions, builds on what you know — and never just hands you the answer.

  • Socratic dialogue — guides you to the answer instead of handing it over
  • Learns your course — your syllabus, your textbook, even how your professor teaches and tests
  • Renders 2D & 3D molecules inline, right in the conversation
  • Voice tutoring “On the Go” — talk through tough concepts; the text stays right there
  • Readiness rings track every exam, problem set, and paper at a glance
