Who it’s for

Built for the people who bid concrete every week

Commercial concrete estimators and self-performing subcontractors bidding schools, retail, warehouses, tilt-up and CMU buildings.

The user

Commercial concrete estimator

Turns invitations to bid into takeoffs and proposals under deadline, across several jobs at once.

Pain: Hours of manual takeoff per bid — and the scope that never made it onto the sheet only shows up at buyout.
The buyer

Owner of a self-performing concrete sub

Wants more bids out the door from the same estimating team, without adding risk to the numbers.

Pain: Hiring estimators is hard, and every missed quantity comes straight out of margin.
The evaluator

Chief estimator / precon lead

A skeptical veteran deciding whether any AI takeoff can be trusted on a real bid.

Pain: AI tools that sound confident and are quietly wrong. ConcreteIQ is built to say when to doubt it.
Benefits

Measured, or flagged. Built so nothing is dropped quietly.

Everything below is built and running in the live beta.

AI takeoff from PDF plan sets

Classifies the sheets, then extracts footings, slab on grade, elevated slabs, tilt panels, site concrete and CMU.

Every scope measured or flagged

Anything it can’t measure with certainty is flagged in front of the estimator instead of silently dropped.

Reads notes and schedules

Treats the wall schedule as a census, reads block-versus-cast-in-place from the callout, and counts “TYP.” details.

Document conflict checks

When the civil and architectural drawings disagree on a covered item, it raises a conflict card to accept or dismiss, and logs RFIs that feed the bid letter.

A priced bid, not a spreadsheet

Rate library, markup cascade and tax, then a customer proposal letter exported to PDF, Word or Excel.

Refuses to finalize unpriced lines

A bid can’t be finalized while any line is unpriced, and a three-step review checks completeness, duplicates and accuracy.

How it works

Upload the plans. Review the takeoff. Send the bid.

  1. Upload your plansStructural, civil and architectural PDFs — the whole set.
  2. AI takes off & flagsQuantities for every concrete scope, with anything uncertain flagged and any conflicts raised.
  3. Review with the guided checksCompleteness, duplicates, accuracy — the estimator stays in charge of every number.
  4. Send a priced bidRates, markup and tax applied; a proposal letter exported to PDF, Word or Excel.
