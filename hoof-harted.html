How it works

Three steps to a verdict

  1. Pick a scanSmell, Smart, Funny — or your own with Pro.
  2. Name the roomType everyone who is playing. The unit has no idea who is there until you tell it.
  3. Point and waitIt sweeps, it thinks out loud, and then it names one of them — at random.

Do

  • Call it a “game” or “party game” everywhere.
  • Keep the GAME label and “Results are randomly generated.” visible whenever a verdict appears.
  • Say plainly that it senses nothing — it picks one of the names you typed, at random.
  • Claim only the five Pro features, exactly as listed.
  • Quote App Store reviews word for word and without the reviewer’s name — there is no agreement to use their names.

Don’t

  • Imply it detects, measures or senses anything — Apple and Google both reject fake-detector claims.
  • Show readouts that describe the room (range, bearing, “3.4 m”).
  • Use numeric content claims (“8 packs”, “200+ sounds”).
  • Use alcohol or drinking in creative, or position it as a dating app.
  • Use any age rating other than 13+, or advertise Android.
  • Call it a “#1 app”.
