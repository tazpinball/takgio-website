The studio

We build the products — not the slide deck.

Takgio isn't a consultancy that advises on AI strategy, and it isn't a dev shop that builds whatever it's asked. We bring deep domain expertise in construction, legal, education, and entertainment to every product — purpose-fit for its industry, built with Claude from Anthropic, and designed for production from day one.

We focus on the industries that stand to gain the most from AI and get the least attention: process-heavy work that generic AI tools don't understand.

That discipline travels. Since March 2026 we've shipped five products — led by ConcreteIQ and Brickture — and the next platform is the one we believe matters most: Meta Ray-Ban Display.
