# Takgio: facts for the phone assistant

Only these facts may be told to callers. Source: takgio.com, 10 November 2026.

## Takgio
- Takgio is a product company based in New Jersey. It turns human expertise into AI products: every product starts with a
  practising expert, whose knowledge is captured and paired with AI.
- President and CEO: Ted Takvorian.
- Website: takgio.com. Email: info@takgio.com.
- Takgio received an ElevenLabs Startup Grant in November 2026.
- Takgio is a member of the Claude Partner Network.

## Products
- ConcreteIQ: AI takeoff and bidding for commercial concrete contractors. It reads a drawing set, takes off the concrete
  scope, flags what it can't measure, and hands the estimator a priced bid. In beta, by invitation. Website:
  concreteiqapp.com.
- Brickture: turn a photo of a real object into a brick model you can build, with step-by-step instructions and a parts
  list. Three tiers of build: Foundation, Ultimate and Ultimate Plus. Launching on the App Store on Thanksgiving,
  November 26, 2026, with Google Play about two weeks later. People can join the Brickture Insiders list on
  takgio.com/brickture for launch news.
- Hoof Harted: a party scan game for iPhone and Android. It picks a "culprit" at random; it detects nothing, that is the
  joke. Free on the App Store and Google Play.
- True Record: an iPhone app that narrates official US government documents, such as Supreme Court opinions and
  congressional reports, word for word. On the App Store. Website: intotherecord.app.
- Ann Elise: an AI tutor for college Organic Chemistry that teaches instead of giving answers. Free to start on the web at
  annelisetutor.com; the iPhone app is in testing.
- Pinball-QR: QR-code problem reporting for pinball and arcade venues. A player scans a card on the machine and the venue
  is emailed. Runs in the browser, no app.
- Coming next: apps for Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses, and myBuyersCoach, a report for home buyers starting in Orange
  County, Florida. Both are in development.
