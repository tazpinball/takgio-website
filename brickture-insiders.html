The Insider’s view

Your world, brick by brick.

Point. Snap. Build it.

Snap a photo of something you love — your dog, your car, your favorite mug — and Brickture designs a brick model of it. It writes the step-by-step instructions, lists every piece, and lets you order the kit. Then you build it.

How it works

From a photo to a model on your shelf

  1. Snap itTake a photo of something you love.
  2. See it in bricksBrickture designs a brick version you can spin around in 3D.
  3. Follow the stepsClear step-by-step instructions, first brick to last, with every piece listed.
  4. Order the kitEvery piece you need, in one box, delivered to your door.
Two ways to build

Pick the build that fits

Foundation

Simple, chunky and fun

A smaller build in standard colors. Great for bold, simple shapes — a mug, a mailbox, a fire hydrant — and a perfect weekend project.

Ultimate

Every detail, every color

Built from your picture, brick by brick, with more colors and more detail. The octopus above is an Ultimate build: 4,224 pieces across 758 steps.

Every model stands on its own brick base.
