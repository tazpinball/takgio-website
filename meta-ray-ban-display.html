Who it’s for

Who a Meta Ray-Ban Display app is for

Meta names the kinds of apps the display is built for: information overlays, real-time data displays, micro-apps, utilities and streaming media. These are the partners we build them with.

Brands & consumer apps

Glanceable companions

Games, transit tools, cooking guides, grocery lists — the Web App examples Meta itself gives.

Pain: A phone app doesn’t translate to a 600×600 heads-up display without a redesign.
Operations

Real-time data, hands-free

Information overlays and live data for people whose hands are busy — from field teams to industrial assistance tools.

Pain: Checking a phone or tablet mid-task costs time and attention.
Accessibility

Assistive experiences

Meta’s own toolkit examples include visual interpreters and object-finding for vision-impaired users.

Pain: Assistive tech that has to be held in a hand.
Benefits

What the platform gives an app

All figures from Meta’s own announcements and developer documentation.

A real display in the lens

Full color, 42 pixels per degree, up to 5,000 nits, with only 2% light leakage.

Silent, subtle input

The Meta Neural Band is an EMG wristband that reads the signals of your muscle activity — up to 18 hours of battery.

Two ways to build

Web Apps that run directly on the glasses, or a phone app that sends UI to the display with Meta’s Device Access Toolkit.

Distribution is coming

Meta says a growing selection of experiences will be browsable via Explore, a new on-glasses app, and in the Meta AI app.

All-day wear

Up to six hours of mixed-use battery life on the glasses.

Early-mover window

Developer access to the display opened on May 14, 2026. Meta says Device Access Toolkit 1.0 begins rolling out September 30, 2026.

How it works

How a partner project works

  1. Pick the glanceable momentThe one thing worth seeing without reaching for a phone.

  4. Build and testThrough Meta’s developer testing channels.
  5. Submit for reviewEvery integration goes through Meta’s review before general release.
